Request for Access to Aggregate Blood Demand Data

Final Year Project  ·  BS Software Engineering, 23rd Batch  ·  Project title: BloodGrid

Section A  ·  Purpose of this request

This document formally requests access to historical, aggregate blood demand records held by your institution, for the sole purpose of academic research undertaken as a Final Year Project in the Department of Software Engineering, FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences, Islamabad.

No patient-identifiable information, donor-identifiable information, or clinical record of any individual is requested or required. The request is limited to counts of blood units by date and blood group, together with a small number of operational indicators described in Section C.

Summary of what is being asked for Daily or monthly counts of blood units issued, broken down by ABO and Rh blood group, covering the longest period your records allow. Where available, we also request indicators of days on which stock was unavailable, so that the analysis can distinguish blood that was requested from blood that was issued.

Section B  ·  The project, and why this data is needed

B1. Project overview

BloodGrid is an academic software project addressing coordination in blood services. It combines two components: matching individual blood requests to suitable registered donors, and analysing historical blood demand in order to anticipate periods of elevated need before they occur.

B2. Why real data is necessary

The second component, blood demand analysis, is the subject of this request. A demand analysis model must be developed and evaluated against genuine historical demand. Synthetic or simulated data can demonstrate that a software system functions, but it cannot establish whether the analysis produces findings of any real value, because the patterns in simulated data are placed there by the person who generated it.

Access to authentic records would allow the project to determine whether demand for particular blood groups follows patterns that can be identified in advance, and whether such a system could give a blood service useful advance notice of periods of elevated demand. Without real data the project can only demonstrate feasibility of the software, not usefulness of the analysis.

B3. Intended benefit to the participating institution

Should your institution wish, the project team will provide, at no cost and with no obligation:

  • A written summary of any demand patterns identified in your data, including seasonal and weekly variation by blood group.
  • A copy of the final project report and its findings.
  • A demonstration of the resulting software system.

The project team makes no claim that its output is validated for operational or clinical use, and no such use is proposed. The work is academic in nature.

B4. Scope limitation

This request relates to historical records only. It does not seek access to any live system, any database connection, any patient record, any donor register, or any operational integration with your institution's systems.

Section C  ·  Data requested

Please supply whatever subset of the following your records permit. Items marked Essential are the minimum needed for the analysis to proceed. Partial data is welcome; the project will state clearly what was and was not available.

C1. Essential fields

Field Description Available
Date Date of issue. Daily records are strongly preferred. Monthly totals are acceptable if daily records are not held.
Blood group ABO and Rh recorded separately, that is A+, A−, B+, B−, AB+, AB−, O+, O−.
Units issued Count of units issued per blood group per period.
Period covered The longest continuous period available. Three years or more is preferred; a shorter period is still useful.

C2. Additional fields, important for accuracy

These allow the analysis to distinguish blood that was needed from blood that was issued. Without them, demand during shortage periods is systematically understated, which is precisely the period the analysis is intended to address.

Field Why it is requested Available
Days at zero stock Identifies periods where demand exceeded what could be issued.
Unfulfilled or referred-out requests Together with units issued, allows actual demand to be reconstructed.
Units cross-matched Cross-match counts sit closer to clinical demand than issue counts.
Units returned unused Distinguishes units issued from units actually transfused.
Units discarded or expired Separates wastage from patient demand.

C3. Optional context

Field Why it is useful Available
Requesting department or ward Separates scheduled surgical demand from emergency demand.
Elective or emergency indicator These two components behave differently over time.
Changes in recording practice Any change of system, capacity, or procedure that would create an apparent step in the records.

C4. Acceptable formats

Comma-separated values, Microsoft Excel, or any standard database export. Consistent column naming across years is helpful but not necessary; the project team will undertake all cleaning and reconciliation. Printed or scanned records can also be accommodated if no electronic form exists, subject to volume.

Section D  ·  Undertakings on data handling and confidentiality

The project team gives the following undertakings in respect of any data provided.

D1. Purpose limitation The data will be used solely for the academic project described in Section B, and for no other purpose, project, or party.
D2. No personal data No patient names, identifiers, record numbers, ages, diagnoses, addresses, or contact details are requested. No donor identifiers are requested. Should any such information be present in a supplied file, it will be deleted before analysis and its presence reported to your institution.
D3. Secure storage Data will be held on password-protected devices belonging to the named team members and the supervising faculty member, and will not be uploaded to any public repository, shared drive, or third-party service.
D4. Aggregate reporting Findings will be reported only in aggregate and statistical form. No individual record will be reproduced.
D5. Attribution Your institution will be named in the project report only with written permission. If permission is withheld, the source will be described generically, for example as "a regional blood centre".
D6. Retention and disposal Data will be retained only for the duration of the project and any subsequent examination process, and will be securely deleted thereafter, or on written request from your institution, whichever is earlier.
D7. Right of withdrawal Your institution may withdraw consent at any time, in writing and without giving reason, upon which all supplied data will be deleted and confirmation provided.
D8. Review before publication On request, your institution will be given the opportunity to review any material referring to its data before submission or publication.
