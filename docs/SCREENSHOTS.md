## Notes for the deck

- Every capture includes the prototype notice at the top. Do not crop it out. It is what keeps
  the slides honest when they circulate without you there to narrate them.
- Screenshots 6, 13 and 15 carry a visible Simulated label. Keep them legible. If a slide shrinks
  them below readable size, add the label as slide text instead.
- The full-page captures (1, 4, 7, 12, 15) are tall. Crop to the top two thirds for a 16 by 9
  slide rather than scaling the whole image down.
- Screenshot 7 is worth a beat of its own if the panel asks about privacy: there is no phone
  number on the donor screen, because the prototype does not hold one.
- If a panel member wants the claim wording in writing, section 4 of
  `docs/SIMULATED_VS_IMPLEMENTED.md` lists the correct and incorrect phrasings side by side.
