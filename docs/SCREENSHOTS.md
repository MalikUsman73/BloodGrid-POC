## Notes for the deck

- **Every capture includes the amber prototype banner.** Do not crop it out — it is what keeps
  the slides honest when they circulate without you narrating them.
- Screenshots 6, 12 and 14 each contain a visible *Simulated* label. Keep them legible; if a
  slide shrinks them below readable size, add the label as slide text instead.
- Full-page captures (1, 4, 8, 10, 14) are tall. Crop to the top two-thirds for a 16:9 slide
  rather than scaling the whole image down.
- If a panel member asks for the claim wording in writing, `docs/SIMULATED_VS_IMPLEMENTED.md`
  §4 lists the correct and incorrect phrasings side by side.
